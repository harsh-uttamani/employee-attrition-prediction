Employee Attrition Prediction

Project Overview & Methodology

What This System Does

This project is an early-warning system designed to identify employees at high risk of leaving the company. By analyzing historical HR data, it uses Machine Learning—starting with a Logistic Regression baseline and culminating in a Random Forest model—to predict whether an employee will Leave or Stay, along with a probability percentage. This empowers HR to make proactive, data-driven retention decisions rather than reacting after a resignation.

The Problem

Replacing trained employees is highly expensive. HR currently lacks a way to foresee attrition risk proactively.

The Objective

Use machine learning on known employee factors to predict future attrition accurately and output actionable insights.

Why Random Forest?

In attrition prediction, successfully identifying potential leavers (Recall) is far more critical than raw accuracy. Random Forest provides significantly higher Recall.

Authentication & Database

MongoDB is integrated into the system to manage company/user registration and login securely. It stores the required authentication information and securely associates users with their company, allowing only authorized administrators to access the Employee Attrition Prediction System.
